## 传递函数

传递函数的标准形式
$$
H(s) =  \frac{K \cdot s}{s^{2} +\frac{\omega_{0}}{Q}s + \omega_{0}^2}
$$

原分母:

$$
1+\frac{R_{1}R_{2}C_{3}+R_{1}R_{2}C_{4}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}\cdot s + \frac{R_{1}R_{2}R_{5}C_{3}C_{4}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}\cdot s^2
$$

化成标准形式
$$
H(s) = \frac{-\frac{C_{3}}{R_{1}}s}{s^{2} + \frac{C_{3}+C_{4}}{C_{3}C_{4}R_{5}} s + \frac{R_{1}+R_{2}}{R_{1}R_{2}R_{5}C_{3}C_{4}}}
$$


> 中心频率

$$
	\omega_{0}^{2 }=\frac{R_{1}+R_{2}}{R_{1}R_{2}R_{5}C_{3}C_{4}} 
$$


如果令$R_{eq} = R_{1}||R_{2} = \frac{R_{1}R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}$, $C_{3} = C_{4} =C$
则有
$$
w_{0} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{ R_{eq} R_{5}}C}
$$

因为$f_{c} = \frac{w_{0}}{2\pi}$

$$
f_{c} = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{ R_{eq} R_{5}}C}
$$

> 品质因数

在前述条件下

$$
\frac{C_{3}+C_{4}}{C_{3}C_{4}R_{5}} = \frac{2}{CR_{5}}
$$
s前的系数应该是$\frac{\omega_{0}}{Q}$
所以有
$$
\frac{\omega_{0}}{Q} = \frac{2}{R_{5}C} = \frac{1}{C\sqrt{ R_{eq} R_{5}}Q}
$$

$$
Q = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{ \frac{R_{5}}{R_{eq}} }
$$


> 带宽

$$
BW = \frac{f_{c}}{Q}
$$
